Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

What we collect

This site has no ads, no tracking cookies and no analytics. The only information we receive is what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email, website link (optional) and message.

How it's used

The contact form is delivered by EmailJS to contact@dksolutions.dev. We use it only to reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote. We don't sell or share it, and we don't add you to a mailing list.

Social media posting

DK Solutions publishes its own short videos to its own Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts using those platforms' official APIs (including the YouTube API Services). These tools only upload our videos and read our own channel details. They don't access, collect or store anyone else's data. YouTube's use is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at Google security settings.

Keeping and deleting

Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to answer you and any work that follows. Email contact@dksolutions.dev to see or delete what we have.
