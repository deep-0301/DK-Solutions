// FAQ

Questions business owners ask about AI.

Will the AI make things up or say the wrong thing?

The assistant answers only from the information you give it: your services, hours, prices and policies. If it doesn't know, it says so and offers to pass the question to you. We test it together before launch.

What about my customers' privacy?

I set things up to follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA): customers are told they're chatting with an AI, only the details needed are collected, and conversations go to you, not into public AI training.

Can it answer in French?

Yes. The assistant can reply in English or French, matching the language your customer writes in. The website itself can be bilingual too.

Can you add AI to the website I already have?

Usually, yes. The assistant can be added to Wix, Squarespace, WordPress, Shopify and most custom sites. If your site is slow or outdated, I'll tell you honestly whether a redesign makes more sense.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. You tell me about your business in plain language and I handle the rest. Updating the assistant later is as simple as sending me the change, or editing a simple document.

Do you only work with businesses in Ottawa?

No. I'm based in Ottawa and happy to meet locally, but I work with small businesses anywhere in Canada over phone and video.

Is the site accessible?

Yes. I build to WCAG 2.1 AA, the standard Ontario's AODA refers to, including keyboard navigation, readable contrast and properly labelled forms, and the chat assistant too.
