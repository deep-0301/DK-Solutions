Welcome to our little corner of the neighbourhood

Crumb & Co. began as a small dream in a home kitchen and has grown over many years into the bakery you see today. Every morning our bakers arrive long before sunrise to shape, proof and bake loaves by hand using slow fermentation and flour from local mills, because we believe good bread takes time and care and attention to detail.

We offer a wide range of sourdough, rye, baguettes, croissants, cinnamon buns, seasonal tarts and celebration cakes, as well as catering for offices and events. Please contact us by phone or visit in store to place an order. Custom cake orders require at least three days notice and a deposit. Gluten free options are available on some days, please ask our staff.

Thank you for supporting a small local business. We look forward to seeing you soon and sharing our love of baking with you and your family for many years to come.

Follow us on all our social channels for news, specials and behind the scenes photos. Sign up to our newsletter at the bottom of this page to receive updates. We also sell gift cards in store, and our bread is available at selected cafes and markets around town. Ask us about wholesale.
